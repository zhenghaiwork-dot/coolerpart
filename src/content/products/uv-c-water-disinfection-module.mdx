This module is intended for enclosed treatment of reservoir or recirculating water in evaporative air coolers. It is a hygiene-support component, not a substitute for filtration, scale control, tank cleaning or scheduled water replacement. Performance depends on UV dose, water clarity, flow, exposure time and maintenance of the lamp and quartz sleeve.
